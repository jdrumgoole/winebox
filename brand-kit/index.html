03 — Colour Palette

Brand Colours

Click any swatch to copy the hex value. The primary palette centres on deep burgundy and warm gold, grounded by natural wood tones.

04 — Typography

Typefaces

Two complementary serif families form the core identity: Playfair Display for headlines and the wordmark, Cormorant Garamond for refined supporting text. DM Sans for body and UI.
