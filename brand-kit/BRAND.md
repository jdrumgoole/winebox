## Brand Overview

**WineBox** is a personal wine cellar collection management app for enthusiasts who want to organize, track, and discover their wine collection with elegance and sophistication.

### Brand Promise
Empower wine collectors to curate, track, and celebrate their collection with confidence.

### Brand Personality
- **Sophisticated yet approachable** – Professional without being stuffy
- **Knowledgeable** – Built by wine enthusiasts for wine enthusiasts
- **Elegant** – Refined aesthetics with attention to detail
- **Empowering** – Tools that make collectors feel capable and informed

## Logo Usage

### Logo Formats

| Format | Purpose | File |
|--------|---------|------|
| **Horizontal Lockup** | Primary logo for web, headers, documents | `winebox_logo_horizontal.svg` |
| **Icon/Symbol** | App icon, favicons, small spaces | `winebox_icon.svg` |
| **Monochrome** | Single-color printing, embroidery, limited-color media | `winebox_logo_monochrome.svg` |

### Minimum Size
- **Web:** 180px wide minimum
- **Print:** 2 inches (5.08cm) wide minimum
- **Icon:** 32px × 32px minimum

### Logo Spacing (Clear Space)
Maintain clear space around the logo equal to the height of the "W" in "WineBox". Do not place other design elements within this space.

### Logo Orientation
- Use horizontal orientation by default
- Icon version only when space constraints require
- Never rotate or skew the logo
- Never add shadows or drop shadows unless specified in design system

### Background Colors
- **Primary:** Use on white or light cream (#FEFEF8) backgrounds
- **Monochrome dark:** Use on white or light backgrounds
- **Monochrome white:** Reserved for dark backgrounds only
- **Minimum contrast ratio:** 4.5:1 (WCAG AA compliance)

### Logo Don'ts
- ❌ Do not change colors (unless using monochrome version intentionally)
- ❌ Do not stretch or distort proportions
- ❌ Do not add gradients or effects
- ❌ Do not place on patterned or busy backgrounds
- ❌ Do not outline or add decorative elements
- ❌ Do not remove or alter the tagline placement
- ❌ Do not use low-resolution raster versions when SVG is available

## Typography

### Font Stack

**Primary Serif (Headings & Logo):**
```css
font-family: "Georgia", "Garamond", serif;
```

**Secondary Serif (Body & UI):**
```css
font-family: "Georgia", "Garamond", serif;
```

**Monospace (Code/Technical):**
```css
font-family: "Monaco", "Courier New", monospace;
```

### Font Weights & Sizes

| Use | Font | Weight | Size | Line Height |
|-----|------|--------|------|-------------|
| **Logo/Hero** | Georgia | Bold (700) | 48-64px | 1.2 |
| **Page Headings** | Georgia | Bold (700) | 32-40px | 1.3 |
| **Section Headings** | Georgia | Bold (700) | 24-28px | 1.4 |
| **Subheadings** | Georgia | Normal (400) | 18-20px | 1.4 |
| **Body Copy** | Georgia | Normal (400) | 16px | 1.6 |
| **Small Text** | Georgia | Normal (400) | 14px | 1.5 |
| **UI Labels** | Georgia | Bold (700) | 12-14px | 1.4 |

### Styling Rules
- Use italic sparingly (taglines, quotes)
- Maintain line-height of at least 1.5 for body text
- Limit color to primary text color (#333333) with exceptions for links (#8B3A3A)
- Avoid underlines except for links

## Imagery & Photography

### Photography Style
- **Color palette:** Warm tones with natural light
- **Composition:** Intimate, detailed shots of wine bottles, glasses, and collections
- **Authenticity:** Real bottles, real collections—avoid overly staged looks
- **Perspective:** Mix of macro (label details), flat-lay (collection shots), and lifestyle (enjoyment)

### Image Grids
- Maintain consistent spacing between images
- Use 4:3 aspect ratio for cards
- 1:1 for gallery collections
- 16:9 for hero/banner images

### Filter & Editing
- Warm color temperature (around 3500-4500K)
- Slight vignette for intimacy
- Enhanced saturation (+10-15%) to bring out wine colors
- Avoid heavy desaturation or cool tones

### Illustration Style
If using illustrations, maintain:
- Simple line work (1-2px stroke)
- Limited color palette (max 4 colors from brand palette)
- Slight imperfection for personality
- Consistent stroke weight

## Voice & Tone

### Brand Voice Characteristics
- **Knowledgeable but not pretentious** – Speak authoritatively without gatekeeping
- **Warm and inviting** – Make collectors feel welcome and confident
- **Clear and helpful** – Provide information in digestible portions
- **Elegant but accessible** – Use sophisticated language thoughtfully

### Tone Examples

**When helping users:**
> "Track every bottle with notes on tasting, price, and perfect occasions. Your collection tells your story."

**When encouraging discovery:**
> "Explore what's in your cellar. Find hidden gems. Plan your next tasting."

**When building community:**
> "Join collectors who take their passion seriously."

### Writing Style
- Use active voice (avoid "can be tracked" → use "track")
- Short sentences (avoid industry jargon without explanation)
- Direct and clear (no marketing fluff)
- Personable (use "you" language)
- Honest about capabilities

### Words to Embrace
- Curate, collection, discover, explore, story, elegance, confidence, passion

### Words to Avoid
- "Proprietary," "disruptive," "synergy," "leverage," "ecosystem"

## Logo Asset Export Guide

### From SVG to PNG (Crisp, Non-Blurry)

#### Method 1: Using Inkscape (Free)
1. Open SVG file in Inkscape
2. File → Export As
3. Set DPI to **300** (for high-quality print/digital)
4. Set width to desired size (e.g., 400px for icon)
5. Export as PNG with **8-bit RGB**

#### Method 2: Using Node.js (for automation)
```bash
npm install -g svgexport

# Export at 2x resolution for web (@2x retina)
svgexport winebox_logo_horizontal.svg winebox_logo_horizontal@2x.png 2x

# Export at 3x for mobile
svgexport winebox_logo_horizontal.svg winebox_logo_horizontal@3x.png 3x
```

#### Method 3: Online Tools (quick, no installation)
- **CloudConvert** (cloudconvert.com)
- **Convertio** (convertio.co)
- **Zamzar** (zamzar.com)

### Export Specifications

#### Web Assets (Digital)
- **Format:** PNG with transparency
- **DPI:** 72 DPI
- **Resolution:** 
  - Icon: 512 × 512px (1x), 1024 × 1024px (2x)
  - Horizontal Logo: 800px wide (1x), 1600px wide (2x)
- **Color Mode:** sRGB
- **File naming:** `winebox_logo_horizontal@2x.png`

#### Print Assets
- **Format:** PNG or PDF
- **DPI:** 300 DPI minimum
- **Resolution:** At least 3000 × 900px for horizontal logo
- **Color Mode:** CMYK (if printing provider requires)
- **File naming:** `winebox_logo_print_300dpi.png`

#### Favicon/App Icon
- **Format:** PNG
- **DPI:** 72
- **Sizes:** 16, 32, 64, 128, 256, 512px (favicon must be square)
- **Background:** Transparent
- **Padding:** 10% clear space on all sides

#### Social Media
- **LinkedIn Company Logo:** 400 × 400px PNG
- **Twitter:** 400 × 400px PNG
- **Facebook:** 500 × 500px PNG
- **Instagram:** 1080 × 1080px PNG

#### Email Signature
- **Width:** 200-250px
- **Format:** PNG
- **Use:** Icon version on left, horizontal logo acceptable
- **DPI:** 72

### Python Script to Generate All Exports
```python

import xml.etree.ElementTree as ET

def export_winebox_assets():
    """Generate all WineBox logo exports from SVG templates"""
    
    # Define export specifications
    exports = {
        'web': {
            'dpi': 72,
            'sizes': [800, 1600],
            'format': 'PNG',
            'color': 'RGBA'
        },
        'print': {
            'dpi': 300,
            'sizes': [3000],
            'format': 'PNG',
            'color': 'RGBA'
        },
        'social': {
            'dpi': 72,
            'sizes': {
                'linkedin': (400, 400),
                'instagram': (1080, 1080),
                'twitter': (400, 400),
            },
            'format': 'PNG',
            'color': 'RGBA'
        }
    }
    
    # Use external tool: cairosvg for SVG rendering
    # pip install cairosvg
    
    from cairosvg import svg2png
    
    # Export horizontal logo at various sizes
    for size in [800, 1600]:
        svg2png(
            url="winebox_logo_horizontal.svg",
            write_to=f"winebox_logo_horizontal_{size}w.png",
            output_width=size
        )
    
    # Export icon at various sizes
    for size in [256, 512, 1024]:
        svg2png(
            url="winebox_icon.svg",
            write_to=f"winebox_icon_{size}x{size}.png",
            output_width=size,
            output_height=size
        )
    
    print("✓ All logo exports generated successfully")

if __name__ == "__main__":
    export_winebox_assets()
```

## What Not to Do

🚫 **Logo Modifications**
- Do not change colors (unless using monochrome intentionally)
- Do not add effects (shadows, glows, 3D)
- Do not rotate, flip, or skew
- Do not stretch disproportionately
- Do not remove elements (bottle, text, tagline)

🚫 **Color Misuse**
- Do not use low-contrast combinations
- Do not invent new brand colors
- Do not desaturate or muddy colors
- Do not apply gradients not in the specification

🚫 **Typography**
- Do not use fonts other than Georgia/Garamond
- Do not change font weights arbitrarily
- Do not justify text (align left/center instead)
- Do not use ALL CAPS for body text

🚫 **Image Quality**
- Do not use low-resolution/pixelated logos (always use SVG or high-DPI PNG)
- Do not compress excessively
- Do not screenshot logos from websites
- Do not scale raster images larger than original
